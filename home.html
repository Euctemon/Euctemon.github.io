Hi

My name is Dave and these are my personal websites where I share some of the things I am interested in. I enjoy clear and beautifully presented explanations, and I try to do the same when writing about a topic I like. The older I become, the more things I am interested in. In high school I was almost exclusively interested in geometry. Then I got to the Czech technical university, where I got captivated by continuum mechanics. To make my life harder, I chose to study biomechanics where nothing is simple. For example, instead of the well-known Hooke's law, I had to deal with hyperelasticity and other nonstandard theories of elasticity, and I loved that.

Then I got back to mathematics at Charles University and studied abstract algebra and type theory, which are entirely different branches of mathematics than those used in engineering fields. I never intended to work as a programmer, yet I was self-learning Haskell during my engineering studies because this functional language was just too interesting to let it pass by me. I continued in learning obscure things in programming and ended up with writing a mathematical proof in a programming language as my thesis.

I find great merit in learning the history and philosophy of science, computers, and mathematics. It sheds light on failed ideas and why the current idea won, how people grappled with certain problems and how they solved them, or if we actually ask the right questions. I am always pleased when a book contains more than just the raw treatment of the topic.
